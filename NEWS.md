# uxlearn 0.1.0

* First release, prepared for CRAN.
* Usability metrics: `ux_task_success()`, `ux_task_time()`, `ux_error_rate()`
  and `ux_sus_score()`.
* Study analysis: `ux_usability_summary()` and `ux_recommendations()`.
* Reproducible simulation of synthetic studies: `ux_simulate_study()`.
* Learning activities, case studies and a workbook (CC BY 4.0).
